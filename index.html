Our Full Story

Founded on the principles of transformative innovation, AUA Technologies is a dynamic private limited company dedicated to shaping the future of global industries. We operate at the vital intersection of technological applications, education, and hands-on training.

Co-founded by a visionary team of cross-border technocrats from India and the United States, our ecosystem is built to address the evolving demands of a digital-first world. By blending technical expertise with academic excellence, AUA Technologies serves as a launchpad for disruptive ideas and sustainable solutions.

We don't just build systems — we nurture the talent required to run them. Through our specialized ventures, we create a seamless bridge between classroom knowledge and industrial execution, ensuring that our technological advancements actively contribute to a smarter, more efficient global economy.

At its core, AUA Technologies is an incubator for progress and purposeful development. Under the strategic guidance of renowned academic luminary Prof. Dr. Urvashi Makkar, we empower the next generation of thinkers, creators, and entrepreneurs. Our ecosystem is uniquely designed to turn bold ideas into scalable, market-ready realities through collaborative mentorship and specialized training programs.

Future Expansion

Global Knowledge
Mobility Centres

AUA Technologies is preparing to establish Global Knowledge Mobility Centres across India, the United States, Europe, and Africa. Operating through strategic international joint ventures, these centres will serve as physical and digital hubs for student mobility, exchange programs, and international start-up mentoring.
